Privacy policy

Last updated {{ updated }}

{{ app_name }} annotates readings you upload. This page explains exactly what happens to your file and what we keep. The short version: we don't keep your files, and we don't have accounts.

Your files

  • Your file is held in the server's memory only while it's being annotated. It is never saved to disk or a database.
  • The annotated PDF is also held only in memory. It is deleted as soon as you download it, or after {{ ttl }} minutes if you don't.
  • We don't keep copies, file names, or the text of your readings, and we don't look at them.

The AI service we use

To decide what to mark, the text of your pages (not the file itself) is sent to Google's Gemini API, which processes it and sends back its suggestions. Google handles that text under the Gemini API terms. Don't upload anything you wouldn't want processed this way, such as private documents or other people's personal information.

What we do keep

To run the free daily limit and to keep an eye on costs, we record numbers only: for each day, how many pages were annotated, how much AI processing that used, and how long it took. These are grouped by an anonymous visitor code, made by scrambling your IP address in a way that can't be reversed. We don't store your IP address itself.

Cookies and tracking

We don't use cookies, advertising, or analytics trackers.

Children

{{ app_name }} is meant for students aged 13 and over. If you're under 18, check with a parent or guardian before using it.
